Getting Started

1

Create an Account

Register using your Gmail and verify your account with OTP to access all Lostify features.

2

Report a Lost Item

Enter item details like title, category, color, location, date, and image to help others identify it easily.

3

Report a Found Item

If you found an item, upload the details so the rightful owner can search and claim it.

4

Track Status

Go to My Status to monitor your submitted reports, matches, and claim progress.

Main Features

Lost Item Reporting

Submit detailed reports for lost items with date, time, place, contact details, and proof of ownership.

Found Item Upload

Users can upload found items with clear descriptions and images so others can locate their belongings.

Smart Search

Search items by title, category, brand, model, color, and location to quickly find possible matches.

Google Login

Users can sign in quickly using their Google account for faster and easier access.

OTP Verification

Gmail-based OTP verification helps confirm users before they create a new account.

Status Tracking

Track all your lost reports, found reports, claim requests, and item matches in one place.

Item Matching

Lostify helps compare lost and found reports to improve the chance of reconnecting items with owners.

Image Upload

Users can upload item photos to improve identification and increase the chances of successful recovery.

Safety Tips

  • Always provide accurate item details and avoid fake claims.
  • Upload clear images whenever possible for better identification.
  • Do not share unnecessary personal details in public descriptions.
  • Double-check item category, location, and date before submitting a report.
  • Use My Status regularly to monitor new matches and updates.
  • Keep your login credentials safe and use verified email access.
